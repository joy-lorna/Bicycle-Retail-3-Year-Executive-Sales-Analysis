# Bicycle-Retail-3-Year-Executive-Sales-Analysis

Bicycle Retail Sales Analytics: Executive Performance Dashboard (2016-2018)

This repository contains a comprehensive **Executive Sales Analysis** for a bicycle retail company covering a three-year period. The project transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence to support executive decision-making.

## 📖 Project Overview
The analysis focuses on identifying growth trends, seasonal patterns, and product performance to optimize inventory and marketing strategies.

[![Kaggle](https://img.shields.io/badge/Kaggle-View_Notebook-00BBFF?style=for-the-badge&logo=kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/code/joylorna/bicycle-retail-3-year-executive-sales-analysis)

**Key Business Questions Addressed:**
* **Revenue Growth:** What is the year-over-year (YoY) growth rate across different regions?
* **Product Mix:** Which bicycle categories (Mountain, Road, Touring) drive the highest profit margins?
* **Customer Demographics:** Who are the high-value customers, and what are their purchasing behaviors?
* **Seasonality:** Identifying peak sales months to improve supply chain efficiency.
